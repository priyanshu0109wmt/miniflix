QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your profile page. Changes take effect immediately and we prorate the difference automatically.

What happens after my free trial ends?

After 7 days, your chosen plan starts billing automatically. We send you a reminder email 2 days before the trial ends, and you can cancel before then without being charged.

How do I cancel?

One click from your profile page. No phone calls, no retention screens. You keep full access until the end of your billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards through Stripe, our secure payment partner. Your card details never touch our servers.

Can I share my account?

That depends on your plan. Basic allows 1 screen, Standard allows 2 screens, and Premium allows 4 screens at the same time.
